NATHAN 2 — asset notices

Engine: Godot 4.7.2, MIT. Full engine and third-party notices: GODOT_ENGINE.txt.
https://godotengine.org/license

Character anatomy and fitted garments: MakeHuman / MPFB standard system assets,
CC0. Models are independently fitted and rigged. MPFB authoring plugin itself
is GPLv3; its code is not distributed in the runtime game.
https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/about.html
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Animation sources: Quaternius Universal Animation Library 2 Standard, CC0.
Additional walk, run, combat, parkour and contact actions are authored for this game.
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary2.html

Coastal surface maps: Poly Haven, CC0:
coast_sand_02, concrete_pavers_03, white_plaster_rough_02, brown_planks_03.
https://polyhaven.com/license

Environment photo references and individual licenses are documented in
assets/environment/SOURCES.md. Reference photographs are not distributed as
game textures. The city is an authored interpretation, not photogrammetry.

Face/tattoo atlases, generic food menu and pine branch spray are generated
illustrative assets. Real private reference photographs are not distributed.
The pine texture is used on intersecting 3D foliage cards, not a tree billboard.

Sound effects: original procedural PCM synthesis in scripts/impact_audio.gd.
Character likeness and unobserved details remain artistic interpretations.
