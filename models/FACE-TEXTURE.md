# Original painted face atlas

Asset: `public/assets/face-atlas.png`. Generated 2026-11-18 with the built-in ImageGen tool. It is a new painted albedo derived from two privately supplied visual references, not a published copy, crop or photo texture. The original reference photographs remain outside this repository. Left panel: Nathan. Right panel: Oz. Oz's glasses remain actual 3D geometry and are excluded from the atlas.

The head, nose, beard and glasses retain volumetric geometry. The generated atlas is projected over the curved front surfaces with feature-based UV coordinates in `src/characters.js`; the back of the head and body use material colour. Missing rear appearances are designed details. This is a stylized interpretation, not photogrammetry or a claim of approved likeness.

The GLB exports embed the PNG with glTF-correct V orientation. Three.js scene JSON exports also embed it. The editable source and this prompt are preserved so it can be replaced or improved.
